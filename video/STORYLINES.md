# Family Three: three storylines for the Hacksgiving film (~55–60 s, 16:9, voice-over)

The one thing: you don't describe the app you want, you pick it out of a litter of three, and the next litter knows you better.
Real data that travels through: the crowd family "Thanksgiving dinner for 8" (thanksgiving-dinner-b468) and the running-tracker family; every screen is the live product.

## A. Dinner for eight (recommended: it is Hacksgiving, and the crowd family really is a Thanksgiving planner)
- Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: object-to-object chain (each shot ends on the thing that opens the next).
- Sees/feels: a lavender, crayon-grain Thanksgiving table; someone needs a dinner planner and has no time to write a spec. They say one line, three working apps appear on the table like dishes, the family at the table votes on their phones, two get crossed, and the keeper is adopted. Warm, funny, very Fly.
- First 2 s: a hand-drawn table in Fly lavender; a phone face-up shows "Dinner is served in 114 minutes" (the real Butter Spine app); VO: "Thanksgiving. Eight guests. Zero plan."
- Beats: hook (table, the need) → tension (a giant prompt box asking for a spec, VO: "You could write a spec…") → reveal (the line "Or raise one." Family Three) → proof (ask → three born at once → vote in Telegram → cross) → payoff (the tree grows to generation 4, every node a live app) → claim → adopt + end card.
- Look: Fly periwinkle ground with risograph grain, serif italic titles, our dark greenhouse UI as the "dishes", coral only on the call to action.
- Sound: warm acoustic-folk bed ~100 bpm that opens up at the reveal; pops on each kid being born, a click per vote, one riser into the tree payoff.

## B. Wildlife documentary (the bold one)
- Device: a character does the work, as a nature film. Form: one continuous take through the greenhouse, zooming into nodes.
- Sees/feels: a hushed nature-documentary narrator watches apps hatch in a greenhouse, compete, cross-breed and leave the nest. It plays the tree like a habitat. Funny and memorable; strong "Weird Uncle" energy.
- First 2 s: dark greenhouse, three app screenshots "hatch" from glowing seed pods; VO whispers: "Here, in the greenhouse, three young apps take their first breath."
- Beats: hatching → the keeper picks one ("natural selection") → cross-breeding → the crowd votes ("the herd decides") → a tree of nine → one leaves the nest (adopt → GitHub repo) → end card.
- Look: our dark greenhouse world, lime and orange light, slow camera, macro punch-ins on real UI.
- Sound: soft orchestral strings and nature ambience, whispered documentary VO, one swell at the payoff.

## C. Stop writing prompts
- Device: type carries the argument, then the product proves it. Form: cut on the beat, then one frame that rebuilds into the tree.
- Sees/feels: giant kinetic type slams the old way (a wall of prompt text, "make it pop", "no, the other blue") then cuts to three apps appearing at once and the tree assembling itself beat by beat. Fast, punchy, social-first.
- First 2 s: a prompt box overflowing with text, words falling off the screen; VO: "You've written this prompt four times."
- Beats: frustration montage → "Raise it instead" → three at once → pick / cross / vote, one per beat → tree builds → adopt → end card.
- Look: black and lime type fields, hard cuts, real UI at 2× scale.
- Sound: driving electronic 120 bpm, hits on every cut, VO crisp and fast.
